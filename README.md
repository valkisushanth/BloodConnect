Blood Connect is a web-based platform designed to connect blood donors with people who need blood during emergencies. The platform aims to simplify the process of finding potential blood donors based on blood group and location.

📌 About the Project

Finding blood donors during medical emergencies can be challenging and time-consuming. Blood Connect provides a centralized platform where donors can register and recipients can search for potential donors.

Our mission is to make blood donation easier, faster, and more accessible.
